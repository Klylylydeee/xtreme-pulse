### Spec changes

- 2026-10-19: Step 2.10 spec (before the build): payroll review, approval and payslips ([Payroll review, approval and payslips (build step 2.10)](docs/modules/talent.md#payroll-review-approval-and-payslips-build-step-210), decisions 350–376).
  - **Review.** Each line carries review flags (onboarding incomplete, timesheet not approved, below minimum wage, net pay below zero, a stale manual entry); HR fixes or acknowledges them with a reason (the new insert-only `payrollFlagAcknowledgements`). Manual entries (the new insert-only `payrollManualEntries`): earnings, deductions (an after-tax one needs the employee's written authorization) and overrides (never of an attendance item), each with a reason and audited, the amount encrypted. A returned run is edited like a draft.
  - **Approval.** HR submits in cut-off order, after a recompute that must change nothing; the approval type `talent.payrollRun` asks any one Board member, who approves or returns it; with no active Board member the submit is refused. A pending approver sees totals, net pay and manual entries after an audited "Show amounts" (HANDOFF item 284 closed without widening the generic reveal, so no Core change).
  - **Finalizing and payslips.** Approval finalizes and locks the run, freezes each payslip's identity, bank account and year-to-date figures, and notifies employees (`talent.payslipReady`); the job `talent.payslipPdfs` renders the PDFs, opened from `/talent/me` through the file route (`talent.payslip`, every open audited). The payroll register lists bank accounts and net pay of a finalized run behind an audited reveal; the Pulse Fiscal posting is a placeholder until step 5.8.
  - **Assumptions** for counsel, the accountant, HR or the user: HANDOFF items 286–318, nine of them marked **Legal** ([Payroll approval and payslips](docs/COMPLIANCE.md#payroll-approval-and-payslips-build-step-210)); owner review items R-263 to R-294.
- 2026-10-19: Step 2.9 spec (before the build): payroll run computation ([Payroll run computation (build step 2.9)](docs/modules/talent.md#payroll-run-computation-build-step-29), decisions 325–349).
  - **Runs.** The new collections `payrollRuns` (one regular run per cut-off, `PAY-` numbers, statuses declared for step 2.10, only `draft` written) and `payrollRunEmployees` (one line per employee). The first payroll cut-off is the first on or after the go-live date. HR creates drafts from `/talent/payroll`; the daily job `talent.payrollDraftRuns` creates the latest one and recomputes every draft; `pnpm dev:payroll-drafts` runs it in development. New notification `talent.payrollDraftReady`.
  - **Pay.** Basic pay with prorating, holiday pay and the workday-before rule, overtime and premiums on the Overtime timesheet only, night differential on paid minutes, allowances with de minimis excess, reimbursements as a separate non-taxable line, attendance deductions from the DTR, SSS (with EC), PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG with the employer shares (the mid-month estimate and the end-of-month remainder), and BIR semi-monthly withholding, with minimum wage earners exempt. The new versioned setting `talent.payrollRunSettings` holds the contribution bases, the monthly-rated special day and the adjustment lookback.
  - **Approval and late changes.** A timesheet not fully approved still gets basic pay; its attendance items settle in a later run by difference against finalized runs, which also settles late leave and holiday changes. The holidays-changed listener recomputes drafts.
  - **Access.** Every amount is encrypted (category `payslip`); Talent Read with HR, Accounting or the System Administrator sees runs, and "Show amounts" and each breakdown are audit-logged as reveals ([Sensitive data](SECURITY.md#sensitive-data)).
  - **Assumptions** for the accountant, counsel, HR or the user: HANDOFF items 225–257, eleven of them marked **Legal** ([Payroll computation](docs/COMPLIANCE.md#payroll-computation-build-step-29)), and 258–285 from the build (none marked Legal).
  - **Built** 2026-10-19 as specified and verified (committed as `0360b68`, not pushed), with the payroll run engine, payroll run settings, payroll run model, payroll run service and payroll drafts job tests in `pnpm test` ([Payroll run tests](docs/TESTING.md#payroll-run-tests)). The pure pay engine is `packages/talent/src/payroll-run/`; the worker registers `talent.payrollDraftRuns` (`0 5 * * *` Asia/Manila) beside the other Talent jobs; the new `pnpm dev:payroll-drafts [--date YYYY-MM-DD]` runs it once in development; and `pnpm seed:admin` adds `talent.payrollRunSettings`. - 2026-10-11: Step 1.10 spec (before the build). New Core collection `holidays` (name, date as the Manila day, derived year, type `regular`, `specialNonWorking` or `specialWorking`, scope `nationwide` or `local` with a free-text location, legal basis, status `draft` or `confirmed`, fixed `source` and `sourceKey`, `declared`, `confirmedAt` and `confirmedBy`), soft-deleted, with a partial unique index on `{ year, sourceKey }` that counts deleted records ([DATA_MODEL.md](docs/DATA_MODEL.md#collection-ownership)). `/holidays` is a Pulse Core page open to every signed-in user, after Approvals in the sidebar; everyone sees confirmed holidays, and only HR and System Administrators see drafts and manage holidays, checked by role on the server ([Holiday calendar](docs/modules/core.md#holiday-calendar), [Rules](SECURITY.md#rules)). The optional online source (`HOLIDAY_SOURCE_URL`, Nager.Date-style JSON) adds missing dates as special non-working day drafts and never confirms ([Environment variables](docs/DEPLOYMENT.md#environment-variables), [Runbook](docs/RUNBOOK.md#online-holiday-source-unreachable)). A legal basis is required to confirm, Confirm all included.
